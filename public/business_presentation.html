Key Takeaways
What the numbers tell us — and what's next
🏆
July 2026: $16,858 all-time record — 41 invoices in a single month. The business is scaling rapidly with one truck.
📈
+82.4% average MoM growth on operational months. Started at $399 in Aug 2025 — now a $200K+ annual run-rate.
💰
73.2% gross margin — well above the 50–60% industry average. High-margin, capital-light business model.
✅
96.2% collection rate on $87,538 billed. Only $3,301 outstanding across 8 open invoices — primarily new July jobs.
🔄
25+ repeat clients including Gibson & Associates and Phoenix Fabricators, each with 9+ invoices. Strong anchor commercial accounts.
📦
15 Yard / 7-Day is the sweet spot — most popular size (40.7%) and highest margin product (74.9%). Pricing is well-calibrated.
🚛
One-truck bottleneck — 41 jobs in July with a single truck likely means turned-down revenue. Phase 1 expansion directly addresses this.
🌟
2026 monthly avg: $10,486/mo — vs $2,947/mo in 2025 (H2 only). 3.6× growth in run-rate in under a year.
🎯
Full-Year 2026 Projection: $150,000 – $170,000
